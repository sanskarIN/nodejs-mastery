# Code of Conduct

> 📘 Book and learning resources: https://ramsandesh.gumroad.com

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on learning and engineering quality. Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

When reporting technical disagreements, discuss evidence, tests, reproducible behavior, and trade-offs rather than attacking people.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that materially harms the community or repository.
